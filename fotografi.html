Landscape Fotografi

Landscape photography is a genre that focuses on capturing images of natural scenes, including mountains, forests, beaches, and other elements of the outdoors. The aim is to showcase the beauty of nature.

Black White Fotografi

Black white photography is an art that highlights contrast between light, shadow. Without color, textures, emotions become clearer. This style is used to express deep feelings, creating powerful images.

Miniature Fotografi

Miniature photography captures small objects to make them appear life-sized, utilizing specialized lenses and techniques to create dramatic effects and highlight intricate details, offering a unique visual perspective.
